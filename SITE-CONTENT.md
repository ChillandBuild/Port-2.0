## 8. His process

Eight stages. The order is real — each depends on the one before it — but nothing here
implies how or whether the sequence should be shown.

1. **Define the ideal customer profile.** Segment the market, map the buyer journey,
   decide who is actually worth reaching.
2. **Research.** Account and market intelligence from Draup, ZoomInfo, Crunchbase and
   direct web research.
3. **Source prospects.** Find contacts and verify them through multi-stage checks before
   anything is sent.
4. **Outreach.** Multi-channel cadences across cold email and LinkedIn, with sending
   domain health monitored.
5. **Qualify.** Track the quality of people who respond, not just the count, and report
   it back to sales and research.
6. **Meetings.** Book discovery calls and demos; connect qualified prospects to account
   executives.
7. **Pipeline management.** CRM data management and funnel reporting, with ROI defined
   before a campaign launches.
8. **Growth.** OKR-aligned production plans, team training and process fixes feed into
   the next cycle.

## 9. Engagement model

A performance-linked structure he offers to clients, in three phases:

1. The client pays only the direct cost of the required tool stack. No markup, no
   retainer at this stage.
2. He does the work: ICP definition, account research, prospect sourcing, and building
   and launching the outreach cadence.
3. Once qualified leads and meetings are being delivered, the client pays for results.
   Payment is linked to conversion, not activity.

**Contact.** Questions about these terms or a booking go through LinkedIn or the phone

**Requesting one.** Contact via LinkedIn or phone, including the booking name, scheduled
