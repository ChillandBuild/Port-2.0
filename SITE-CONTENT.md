## 8. His process

Eight stages. The order is real — each depends on the one before it — but nothing here
implies how or whether the sequence should be shown.

1. **Define the ideal customer profile.** Segment the market, map the buyer journey,
   decide who is actually worth reaching.
2. **Research.** Account and market intelligence from Draup, ZoomInfo, Crunchbase and
   direct web research.
3. **Source prospects.** Find contacts and verify them through multi-stage checks before
   anything is sent.
4. **Outreach.** Multi-channel cadences across cold email and LinkedIn, with sending
   domain health monitored.
5. **Qualify.** Track the quality of people who respond, not just the count, and report
   it back to sales and research.
6. **Meetings.** Book discovery calls and demos; connect qualified prospects to account
   executives.
7. **Pipeline management.** CRM data management and funnel reporting, with ROI defined
   before a campaign launches.
8. **Growth.** OKR-aligned production plans, team training and process fixes feed into
   the next cycle.

## 9. Engagement model

A performance-linked structure he offers to clients, in three phases:

1. The client pays only the direct cost of the required tool stack. No markup, no
   retainer at this stage.
2. He does the work: ICP definition, account research, prospect sourcing, and building
   and launching the outreach cadence.
3. Once qualified leads and meetings are being delivered, the client pays for results.
   Payment is linked to conversion, not activity.

and implementation.

**Contact.** Questions about these terms or a booking go through LinkedIn or the phone
number on this site.

### Privacy policy

**Information collected.** Depending on how someone makes contact or books: name, contact
details, company information, booking preferences, consultation notes they choose to
share, and payment or refund status. This site does not ask for or store full card
numbers, CVV codes, or banking passwords.

**How it is used.** To reply to enquiries and provide requested information; to schedule,
deliver, reschedule or refund a consultation; to maintain transaction and service
records; to improve the clarity and reliability of the site and service.

**Payment and service providers.** Where a payment provider is used, payment details are
entered on that provider's secure page under its own privacy policy. Only the information
needed to confirm the transaction and deliver the service is used by Sampath Kumar. No
physical shipping applies.

**Sharing and retention.** Information may be shared with service providers assisting with
scheduling, communications, payment processing or video calls. It is not sold as a mailing

dispute handling, or legal obligations.

**Your choices.** You may ask what personal information is held, request correction of
inaccurate information, or ask a service-related question. Some records may need to be
retained for accounting, security or legal reasons.

**Contact.** Privacy questions go through LinkedIn or the phone number on this site.

### Refund and cancellation policy

**Cancelling before the session.** A full refund is available when the client cancels at
least 24 hours before the scheduled start time, requested through the same contact method
used for booking.

**Rescheduling.** One reschedule may be requested at least 24 hours before the session,
subject to availability, normally within 30 days of the original booking.

**Late cancellation or no-show.** Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the
session, or a missed session without notice, may not qualify for a refund. A reschedule
may be offered at his discretion.

**If he cancels.** The client may choose a replacement time or receive a full refund for
the undelivered session.

**Refund processing.** Approved refunds go to the original payment method through the
payment provider. Timing depends on the provider and the client's bank or card issuer.
No physical shipping applies.

**Requesting one.** Contact via LinkedIn or phone, including the booking name, scheduled
time, and payment reference if available.
