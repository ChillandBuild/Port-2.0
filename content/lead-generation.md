LEAD GENERATION WITH DRIP CAMPAIGN METHODOLOGY Drip Campaign Structure
Our lead generation approach centers on giving equal importance to
content and engagement tracking. The strategy follows a structured drip
campaign model, ensuring prospects receive timely, relevant information
that guides them from awareness to conversion. I use a minimum of seven
sequences to build consistent communication with potential leads. Each
sequence has a specific purpose: 1.Why we are reaching out -- Explain
the intent behind our communication and the value we aim to bring. 2.Who
we are -- Introduce our company, core values, capabilities, and
expertise. 3.How we can help -- Describe how our products or services
can improve productivity or solve business challenges. 4.Case studies --
Share real-world success stories that demonstrate measurable impact.
5.Portfolio -- Present a visual and detailed overview of completed
projects or key clients. 6.Product or Service Decks -- Offer a deep dive
into our solutions and deliverables. 7.Follow-up and Call to Action --
Encourage responses, meetings, or demos to move the lead forward. This
sequence ensures a natural storytelling flow built on trust and value
creation.
